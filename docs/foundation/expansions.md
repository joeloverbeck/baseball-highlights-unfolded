# Expansion requirements

The Reference Booklet defines timing across the bundled packs. A card’s printed conditions narrow these rules. Keep each pack selectable when its required assets and behavior are verified. Distinguish an immediate hit, a change to a pending threat, a later trigger, and a series or Buy Round modifier in the engine and on screen.

A Free Agent check reveals the top card, compares its type and returns it to the bottom. Preserve that change to deck order on success and failure. For two Immediate Actions the manager chooses the order when both qualify. Check each condition in context. **DT-587** can cancel a second hit only when its first Glove actually cancels a home run.

## Coaches and parks

Brawl removes the opposing top player and its threats before your play. Replace it from the free-agent deck, applying only its boxes. Double Steal advances one chosen runner two bases. Triple Play removes up to three opposing runners, including fast ones. Bullpen preemptively cancels the specified opposing type’s boxes. Scout temporarily exposes three additional agents for your buy, then shuffles unbought extras back. Fan Favorite adds five revenue. Base Running sets the specified type to fast for this game. Steal Signs grants a one-time look at the opposing hand before your play. Bench grants all your players PH, Batting appends a single for the specified type, and Defense grants your Robots Glove for the mini-game.

## All-Stars and promos

A converted Bunt moves batter and runners one base for each converted hit, ignoring speed bonuses. Golden Glove cancels three hits, Leaping Catch targets up to two homers, and a Robot check can threaten a cycle in single-to-homer order. Distinguish explicit OR choices from combined effects. Magna Glove Plus may clear runners, grant later Naturals a Glove or append a selected cancelled hit. Save eligibility follows the particular rider. A printed opponent’s choice assigns the choice to the opposing seat, including immediate homer versus six revenue. A batter-matching check can combine cancelling threats and clearing runners. Steal Signs adds a final conditional double that can disappear when the next opposing type is revealed. A protected contract excludes demotion. Later Cyborg doubles and Robot singles append after other effects for that mini-game. A copy limited to one action from the previous card cannot copy both halves.

Promo variants protect printed homers against Cyborg cancellation, reorder the manager’s top three before an optional PH, grant four revenue once per series or refresh up to three market cards once per series. A successful Wiffle Robot check against Knuckleball preserves three singles and adds one revenue. High Heat checks for cancellation with a walk fallback. The combined catch/clutch requires trailing with a runner for the clutch homer. Some Magna Gloves append both cancelled hits. Opponent’s Choice can exchange cancellation for refreshing up to two hand cards, or clearing the opponent’s bases for three walks. Neither choice card works as a Visitor Save. Sweep cancels only when the score differs; Sweep Plus can append a cancelled hit. Triple Crown executes an immediate homer on a Natural check.

For each card record target seat, timing, condition, choices, zones, duration, ordering, Save eligibility and source. Verify conditions on both sides of their boundary, including tied scores and failed checks. Keep unlocated promo and All-Star faces in the content ledger until source and behavior can be verified.
