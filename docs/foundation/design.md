# Ballpark, interface and art direction

Build an Ubuntu-native 3D ballpark where a manager can read both sides’ game state and see baseball happen. Preserve the actual printed faces and common back. Let the design rounds determine camera, framing, lighting, scale and visual style. Optimize the view around the next meaningful decision. At a Glove decision, show which threats can be stopped and the projected consequences. At a buy, keep budget, candidate, cost, immediate refill and demotion legible together.

Distinguish threatened hits from resolved action. A batter can wait at home while the opponent responds; after resolution the ball can fly and runners advance in order. Support quick completion of motion and a stable final state. Never hide a legal decision under an animation. A projection must account for board position without revealing private cards. White, blue and red runners need distinguishable silhouettes or speed marks at playing scale. Runner speed and Natural/Robot/Cyborg are separate dimensions.

For each new 2D graphic, present at least three competent, materially different alternatives made in VectorCraft. For each layout or composite UI, make at least three alternatives using both VectorCraft and FigCraft. Compare them at playing size on the same position. For each manager decision, recommend a direction and explain what each alternative makes easier or harder to see or do. When different designs serve different decisions best, show how their strongest parts work together before asking the owner to select. Show the owner the alternatives and obtain selection before application implementation. Effects belong in EffectCraft when motion is being authored. Preserve editable source and inspect the selected result in the native scene.

Before reviewing a reused art export, reproduce it from the editable source and compare it with the candidate asset. Record the source path, tool version, and export command or settings in the existing handoff.

For new 3D pieces agree on the piece’s role and scale, then create consistent front, back, left and right turnaround portraits when modeling requires them. Maintain the same proportions, orientation convention, materials and base across views. Choose Blender modeling or Tripo with the owner for that asset. Tripo submission, external uploads and spend are separate from local design. Inspect the resulting piece from the game camera, alongside the other speeds and the cards, and let the owner judge the combined presentation.

## Selected direction for Glove and the Buy Round

The owner selected the [combined manager study](../../art/manager-review/COMBINED.md) on 2026-11-27. Use a high field camera for live decisions, warm scorecard panels for comparing consequences, and player art in six stable market slots. A low camera can follow a resolved hit before the next decision. The [review handoff](../../art/manager-review/HANDOFF.md) supplies the two positions, the editable study restoration steps and the shared-code trace. That study left the amount of printed information to repeat beside each offer open for native evaluation.

After reviewing the native app maximized, the owner selected the [Press Box market row](../../art/manager-review/signing/review.html), the autograph **Sign** action and the [nearby card symbol explanation](../../art/manager-review/symbols/review.html). The six readable faces and their prices are the offers. Hover or keyboard focus explains only that player's speed and any PH mark, and shows the cost and remaining balance during a purchase. The same marks have the same explanation in hand and In Play. Each decision supplies its own legal actions.

For the [revised combined match](../../art/manager-review/match/combined.html), selected on 2026-11-28, keep the stadium anchored beside a grouped hand. At a Glove decision the hand gives way to both source faces and a side-by-side comparison of cancellation, cause, score and occupied bases. Keep the live field in place until the manager commits. Put the Home pennant by its team on the scoreboard. Separate current runs, series wins, viewer and acting manager. Leave a visible gap between the hand-card highlight and **Play**, and keep the action reachable as the pointer crosses it. The combined native layout and input remain to be verified at the owner's maximized desktop size.

The [selected between-games study](../../art/manager-review/between-games/combined.html) uses each team's itemized revenue with the contributing face available for inspection. Keep earned revenue, available funds, spending and expiry distinct. When a buyer owes a demotion, give their eligible In Play faces priority, keep the six market slots visible, and move the chosen card face down to the labeled Minor Leagues. A waiting manager sees the current buyer and that team's funds. After both finish, show how the Lineup and Dugout make up the active roster. Let either manager inspect the public Dugouts. Give the entitled manager a prominent **Start Game 2** action with the host, first player and series standing visible.

## Authoring tools

Start a trusted Codex session or Claude Code from the project root. The project configuration registers VectorCraft, FigCraft, EffectCraft and PhotoCraft by game name, reads the checkout and writes editable art under art/. Use PhotoCraft for [card extraction](content.md#extract-card-artwork-in-photocraft). Blender uses the shared local Blender MCP bridge (mcp-for-blender 2.3.1, uvx, Python 3.11). Enable the companion in the intended Blender session before scene work. Reopen the project client to load newly added MCP definitions. Keep private reference imports and exports under owner custody.
