## Rulings to reconcile before later packs

Mike Fitzgerald recommends a dedicated row of ten All-Stars/promos. His preferred variant gives each manager one or two pre-series selections, by draft or agreed budget. Replace one starter per superstar and reduce the opening buys by the number of superstar drafts. He also describes making the special row available twice during a seven-game series. Record the chosen variant. Ralph H. Anderson confirms that AS-596/597/598 affect subsequent qualifying cards in the same mini-game. They cannot trigger on themselves or carry into the next game. These original posts and follow-ups were verified from BoardGameGeek’s public article records. [Designer setup](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3440256/how-to-use-the-new-all-star-expansion-and-promos-i) · [Roster and buys](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3440256/article/45495030) · [Ongoing effects](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3446826/as-whenever-you-play-precision-as-596597598) · [All-Star FAQ](https://boardgamegeek.com/filepage/293610/all-stars-and-promos-rules-and-faq) · [Publisher final promo files](https://gamefound.com/en/projects/eagle-gryphon-games/baseball-highlights-2045-bases-loaded/updates/7)

For later multiplayer work use RB 13 for the four-player tournament. For Hot Pepper consult the standalone two-page rules alongside Deluxe pp. 9–10. Tournament pairings, separate markets and standings and the three-sided game’s direction, final save and tie handling each require their own spec. Optional external fixtures include [Guided Play](https://boardgamegeek.com/filepage/148034/bh2045-guided-play) and [Tournament Guidelines](https://boardgamegeek.com/filepage/115037/baseball-highlights-2045-tournament-guidelines).

BoardGameGeek listed the supplementary FAQ, Guided Play, standalone Hot Pepper and Tournament Guidelines as files 409267, 189720, 120368 and 140653. Its SHA-256 matches the private copy in the rules folder.
