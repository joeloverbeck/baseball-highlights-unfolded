# Baseball Highlights: 2045

A manager takes a team through a series of mini-games. These terms describe where players go during a game and between games.

## Cards between games

**Buy Round**

The period when managers spend the revenue their **In Play** players earned to sign free agents. The lower-earning team chooses who buys first. On a tie, the team that lost the mini-game chooses.

**Buy Row**

The face-up free agents available to either team. A signing immediately opens the same slot for a replacement from the **Free Agent Deck**.

**Free Agent Deck**

The shared face-down supply for the **Buy Row**.

**Lineup**

A team's face-down draw pile. A newly signed player goes on top.

**In Play**

The team's stack of played cards. These players contribute revenue and supply the eligible demotions during the **Buy Round**.

**Minor Leagues**

The face-down pile of players demoted from a team's active roster.

**Dugout**

A team's face-up discard pile, which both managers may inspect. It receives the players still **In Play** after buying. A required draw from an empty **Lineup** returns the shuffled **Dugout** to the **Lineup**.
